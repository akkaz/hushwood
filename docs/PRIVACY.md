# Data and classroom use

The microphone's waveform is processed inside the teacher's browser using Web Audio. Hushwood does not record, upload, transcribe or identify voices. The level is a relative signal from the current microphone, not calibrated decibels. Position, device gain and room acoustics affect it: calibrate in each room.

The public playground needs no account. Its game progress belongs to that session. School mode stores teacher account details, school/class names and aggregate lesson statistics (duration, quiet seconds, best streak, XP). Do not enter pupil names or other personal information in class names or reward text. There are no pupil accounts.

Teachers should agree on a reasonable threshold and breaks with their class. A loud moment interrupts a streak; it never removes earned lifetime XP. Rewards and consequences should be supportive, collective and age appropriate. This is a classroom activity, not a disciplinary assessment or a measurement of individual behavior.

A school invite is an access credential: share privately with colleagues. An email domain rule is an additional filter, not proof of employment or mailbox ownership. Sign-in is provided by ChatGPT Sites. Hushwood does not handle passwords or send verification email. Contact the installation administrator for account support. Keep school comparisons private and interpret results in context: the browser measurement can be manipulated and rooms differ.

For self-hosting, configure your own retention/deletion policy and contact channel before inviting real schools. ChatGPT Sites and its Cloudflare D1 hosting infrastructure process the stored account and aggregate data under the operator's settings. The public build is an open-source prototype, not a claim of regulatory certification.
