Small sounds. Private by design.

The microphone is processed in your browser. Hushwood does not record, upload, transcribe or identify voices. Only the current sound level controls the creature.

Play without an account

The public game needs no account. Your session's progress stays in the browser's memory. Your language preference is stored on this device. A clearly labeled simulation works without microphone access.

Play with your school

ChatGPT Sites provides teacher sign-in. School mode stores school and class names, membership, reward settings and aggregate lesson statistics: measured seconds, quiet seconds, longest streak and XP. There are no student accounts. Do not enter pupil names or personal information in class names or reward text.

School data is accessible to authorized members, not anonymous visitors. Invitations are private access codes. An optional email domain rule is an organizational filter, not proof of employment or verified mailbox ownership.

Use it thoughtfully

Sound levels are relative to the microphone, not scientific decibels. Agree on a reasonable threshold, calibrate in each room and make room for discussion, breaks and different needs. Noise can interrupt a streak; earned lifetime XP is never removed. This is a collective activity, not an individual disciplinary assessment.

School cups compare aggregate quiet ratios, subject to a minimum measured time. Room acoustics, devices and teacher settings differ. Browser measurements can be manipulated, so do not use the results for high-stakes rankings.

Hosting and requests

This installation runs on ChatGPT Sites and Cloudflare D1. The hosting platform may process technical request logs and sign-in information under its own policies. Hushwood includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

For questions or deletion requests, contact the project maintainer through the Hushwood repository. Do not put private school details in a public issue. Self-hosters must establish their own retention and contact policies before inviting schools.
